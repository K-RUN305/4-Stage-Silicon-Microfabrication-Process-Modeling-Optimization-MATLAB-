# 4-Stage-Silicon-Microfabrication-Process-Modeling-Optimization-MATLAB-
Developed a MATLAB model for RCA cleaning, thermal oxidation, photolithography, and wet/dry etching. Applied Deal–Grove and Dill’s models to optimize oxide growth and lithographic resolution; compared wet etching and RIE for anisotropy and CD control.
Developed a MATLAB-based 4-stage microfabrication model comprising RCA cleaning, thermal oxidation, photolithography, and wet/dry etching.
Applied Deal–Grove oxidation kinetics to model SiO₂ growth and optimize oxide thickness at different temperatures.
Modeled photolithographic exposure using Dill’s model and evaluated resolution and depth-of-focus using Rayleigh criteria.
Compared isotropic wet etching and anisotropic Reactive Ion Etching (RIE) through etch-rate, selectivity, anisotropy, and critical-dimension analysis.
Achieved 100 nm SiO₂ thickness with <1.2% variation, 0.35 μm lithographic resolution, and RIE anisotropy of 0.92 with 0.015 μm CD loss.
